About

Seventeen apps, one stubborn idea.

I'm an iOS engineer on the founding team at Ultrahuman. Before that: Zomato, LimeTray, Zolo.

Apple gave me a WWDC Student Scholarship in 2016 and I've written Swift most days since. All of it was built after hours, by one person, with no team and no funding.

One idea runs through all of it. Build native. Compute on the device. Send nothing home.

Native, or not at all

SwiftUI and UIKit, Metal where it earns its keep, and Apple Watch, widgets, Live Activities and Vision Pro built properly rather than ticked off.

The data is yours

Storage on your device, sync through your own iCloud if you want it. No account to make, nothing to log into, no server holding your history.

Honest about what it knows

When an app makes a claim about your body or your money it shows its working: the sample size, the confidence, and the reasons it might be wrong.

Say hello.

Happy to talk about any of it: the apps, the design decisions, or the parts that didn't work.
