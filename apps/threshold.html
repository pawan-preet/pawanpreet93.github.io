The app

Zones anchored to your threshold, not to 220 minus your age.

Heart-rate zone training for Apple Watch and iPhone, built on lactate threshold rather than the borrowed percentage-of-maximum model most zone apps still use.

The incumbent in this category puts custom zone boundaries behind a paywall and derives them from a formula that was never meant to be precise. Threshold inverts both. Accessibility, dark mode and localisation were in scope from the first commit.
