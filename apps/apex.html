The app

The race weekend, told properly.

A Formula 1 companion: live timing with sector splits, championship standings, circuit maps that mark the overtaking zones, tyre stints, and driver and constructor profiles carrying a whole career rather than eleven rounds of one.

Hand-drawn track maps, a reaction-lights game, season history, widgets and an Apple Watch app. An independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Formula 1.
