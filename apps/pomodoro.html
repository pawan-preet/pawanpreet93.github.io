The app

Deep focus, one tomato at a time.

A native focus timer built on the Pomodoro technique: twenty-five minute sprints, breaks you actually take, and a task list light enough to keep using. Streaks and heatmaps make the effort visible without turning it into a game you can lose.

The timer lives everywhere the system allows. Home and Lock Screen widgets, Live Activities with Dynamic Island, a full Apple Watch app with complications, and Siri Shortcuts. You can start a session and steer it without ever opening the app.
