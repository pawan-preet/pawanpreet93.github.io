The app

A calmer set of car controls.

Climate, charging, locks and location for an electric car, plus a tyre-pressure watch and a history view. Built because the official app buries the four things you use daily behind everything else.

A personal project rather than a store submission. Not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Tesla, Inc.
