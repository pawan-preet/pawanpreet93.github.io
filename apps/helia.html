The app

Sunlight, measured.

A vitamin D tracker that estimates what you actually synthesised, from the sun's real angle at your location, your skin type, what you were wearing and how long you stayed out.

The hero is a living sky that follows the weather and the hour. Every figure is presented as an estimate and carries a medical disclaimer, because that is exactly what it is. Your location is rounded to about a kilometre before anything is calculated.

Privacy

Health data is read live and never synced. Your location is coarsened to roughly a kilometre.

Third partiesNetwork
Apple WeatherKit — receives your coarse (~1 km) location to fetch weather and UV forecasts; RevenueCat — validates App Store purchases; receives the store receipt and a random anonymous app id, never your name, email, health data or location; Tally — only if you choose to open a feedback form in Settings; nothing is sent unless you submit the form
