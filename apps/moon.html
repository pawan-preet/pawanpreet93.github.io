The app

The real moon, exactly as you see it.

A photorealistic lunar sphere rendered in Metal and lit at tonight's true phase. Spin it like a globe, drag a timeline to watch it wax and wane through any date, then pull up a glass drawer of precise figures: illumination, moonrise and moonset, distance, altitude and azimuth.

A single on-device astronomy engine built on Meeus' algorithms feeds every surface: iPhone, iPad, Mac, Vision Pro, Apple Watch, widgets and complications. The moon is oriented to your sky rather than to a stock illustration, at your latitude, on your date.
