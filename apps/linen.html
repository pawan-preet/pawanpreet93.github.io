The app

A quiet archive for the looks you love.

Save the fashion images you like, usually screenshots, and Linen trims the app chrome away, reads the caption, pulls the outfit's colours and files each look into a masonry wall and a shelf of linen-bound seasonal volumes.

Built to feel like a case-bound book rather than a utility: bone paper, warm charcoal ink, square corners, hairline rules. Your photographs are the only real colour in the app. All the vision work runs on the device and the original screenshot is discarded once it has been trimmed.
