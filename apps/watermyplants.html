The app

Never let a plant go thirsty.

Watering reminders sized to each plant instead of a generic weekly nag. Add a plant, say how much it drinks and how often, and the app remembers the rest.

The oldest app here and the one that reaches furthest back, still supporting iOS 16. Rebuilt around a Liquid Glass redesign with photo-forward cards, an Apple Watch app and a widget. Ships in English and Hindi.
