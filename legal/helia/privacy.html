Helia

Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 August 2026

Helia is built by Pawanpreet Singh. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It is short because the app does little: health data is read live and never synced. Your location is coarsened to roughly a kilometre.

What is collected

Helia uses the third-party services listed below. Apart from those, no personal data is collected by me, and there is no advertising.

Where your content lives

SwiftData on-device, with opt-in private iCloud sync. Data held in iCloud is stored in your own Apple account, under Apple's terms, and I cannot read it.

Health data

HealthKit is read live on-device and never uploaded. Health data is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.

Location

Coarse location, rounded to ~1 km, used for solar geometry. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

Purchases

If you buy Helia Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple. The app uses RevenueCat to validate purchases and manage subscription status: it receives your App Store receipt and an anonymous, app-generated identifier. RevenueCat never sees your name, email, health data or location, and this information is not used for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Feedback

The optional feedback form in Settings is hosted by Tally. Only what you choose to type into the form is sent, and only when you submit it. See Tally's privacy policy.

Third-party services

  • Apple WeatherKit — weather and UV forecasts for your coarse location
  • RevenueCat — purchase validation (see Purchases above)
  • Tally — the optional feedback form (see Feedback above)

Children

Helia is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because your content stays on your device or in your own iCloud, you are already in control of it: you can view, edit, export or delete it in the app, and deleting the app removes its local data. If you have a question about your data, email me and I will answer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new date.
