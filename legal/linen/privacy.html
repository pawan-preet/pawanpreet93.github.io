Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 August 2026

Linen is built by Pawanpreet Singh. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It is short because the app does little: no network calls at all. Images never leave your device unless you export them.

What is collected

Nothing is collected by me. Linen has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. I receive no personal data from your use of the app, and I have no way to identify you.

Where your content lives

Images stored on-device in the app group; opt-in iCloud. Data held in iCloud is stored in your own Apple account, under Apple's terms, and I cannot read it.

Third-party services

None. The app contains no third-party SDKs, no trackers and no advertising networks.

Children

Linen is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because your content stays on your device or in your own iCloud, you are already in control of it: you can view, edit, export or delete it in the app, and deleting the app removes its local data. If you have a question about your data, email me and I will answer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new date.
