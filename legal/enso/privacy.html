Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 August 2026

Enso is built by Pawanpreet Singh. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It is short because the app does little: no bank linking and no accounts. Your spending is computed on your device and syncs only through your own private iCloud; what leaves it is an anonymous exchange-rate lookup and, if you buy Pro, the purchase check that validates it.

What is collected

Enso uses the third-party services listed below. Apart from those, no personal data is collected by me, and there is no advertising.

Where your content lives

SwiftData on-device, with opt-in private iCloud sync. Data held in iCloud is stored in your own Apple account, under Apple's terms, and I cannot read it.

Location

Optional. When you turn on location suggestions, your location is read on the device to pre-pick a category for where you are standing. It is never uploaded and never leaves the device. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

Third-party services

  • Frankfurter and Open Exchange Rates — receive the currency codes to convert, nothing else; no account, no key, no identifier, and no spending data
  • RevenueCat — validates App Store purchases; receives the store receipt and a random anonymous app id, never your name, email, or any transaction, budget or category
  • Tally — only if you choose to open a feedback form, the link carries the app version, build, OS version and device model so reports are actionable; nothing is sent unless you submit the form

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. I never see your payment details. Purchase status is used only to unlock features you have paid for.

Children

Enso is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because your content stays on your device or in your own iCloud, you are already in control of it: you can view, edit, export or delete it in the app, and deleting the app removes its local data. If you have a question about your data, email me and I will answer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new date.
