Flux

Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 August 2026

Flux is built by Pawanpreet Singh. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It is short because the app does little: no account, no login, ever. Its privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data.

What is collected

Nothing is collected by me. Flux has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. I receive no personal data from your use of the app, and I have no way to identify you.

Where your content lives

A single JSON file in the app's own container. No cloud sync. It does not leave your device unless you export or share it yourself.

Health data

Optional two-way Apple Health for water and sleep. Health data is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.

Third-party services

None. The app contains no third-party SDKs, no trackers and no advertising networks.

Children

Flux is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because your content stays on your device or in your own iCloud, you are already in control of it: you can view, edit, export or delete it in the app, and deleting the app removes its local data. If you have a question about your data, email me and I will answer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new date.
