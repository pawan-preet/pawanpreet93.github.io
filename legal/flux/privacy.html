Flux

Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 August 2026

Flux is built by Pawanpreet Singh. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It is short because the app does little: no account, no login, ever. Its privacy manifest declares no tracking and no collected data.

What is collected

Nothing is collected by me. Flux has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. I receive no personal data from your use of the app, and I have no way to identify you. The one exception is purchase processing, described under Third-party services below — and that never touches what you drink.

Where your content lives

A single JSON file in the app's own container. No cloud sync, and no server of mine. It does not leave your device except through the optional Apple Health sync you turn on yourself.

Health data

Optional, and off until you turn it on. Flux writes the water and caffeine you log to Apple Health. With your permission it reads water and caffeine (so drinks logged in other apps appear here), body weight and biological sex (to suggest a daily goal), and sleep, resting heart rate, workouts, steps, active energy and walking & running distance (to show how your caffeine relates to your sleep and your day). Health data stays on your device: it is never sold, never shared with third parties, never sent to me, and never used for advertising. You can revoke access at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.

Third-party services

One. Flux uses RevenueCat to process and validate Flux+ purchases. When you open a purchase screen or buy Flux+, RevenueCat receives a randomly generated app user ID along with the purchase and device information needed to check your subscription status. That ID is not linked to your name or email, and no hydration, caffeine or Apple Health data is ever sent to it. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Beyond that there are no analytics SDKs, no trackers and no advertising networks.

Children

Flux is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because your content stays on your device, you are already in control of it: you can view, edit and delete it in the app, and deleting the app removes its local data. Anything Flux wrote to Apple Health stays in Health and can be deleted there. If you have a question about your data, email me and I will answer.

Changes
