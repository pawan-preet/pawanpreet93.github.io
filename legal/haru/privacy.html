Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 August 2026

Haru is built by Pawanpreet Singh. This policy explains what the app does with your information. It is short because the app does little: no account, no network, no feed.

What is collected

Nothing is collected by me. Haru has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. I receive no personal data from your use of the app, and I have no way to identify you.

Where your content lives

On-device. It does not leave your device unless you export or share it yourself.

Third-party services

None. The app contains no third-party SDKs, no trackers and no advertising networks.

Children

Haru is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Because your content stays on your device or in your own iCloud, you are already in control of it: you can view, edit, export or delete it in the app, and deleting the app removes its local data. If you have a question about your data, email me and I will answer.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new date.
